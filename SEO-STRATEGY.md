# KMC Loader Scales - SEO Strategy & Implementation Guide

## Overview
This document outlines the SEO improvements made to maximize Google discovery for your loader scales business targeting Australian industries and regions.

---

## ✅ What We've Implemented

### 1. **Industry Pages** (High-Value Keywords)
Each page targets specific industries with unique content:

- **Mining & Quarries** (`mining-quarries.html`)
  - Target keywords: mining loader scales, quarry scales, heavy loader scales
  - Content: Mining operations, extraction processes, rugged design focus
  
- **Agriculture** (`agriculture.html`)
  - Target keywords: farm scales, silage scales, agricultural loader scales
  - Content: Silage, hay, grain tracking, remote area farming
  
- **Construction** (`construction.html`)
  - Target keywords: construction loader scales, earthmoving scales
  - Content: Material tracking, contractor solutions, project management

### 2. **Location Pages** (Regional Keywords)
Each page targets Australian regions where you operate:

- **Queensland** (`queensland.html`) - Your HQ focus
  - Target keywords: Queensland loader scales, Brisbane scales
  - Content: Regional mining, agricultural, construction focus
  
- **NSW** (`nsw.html`)
  - Target keywords: NSW loader scales, New South Wales scales
  - Content: Agriculture & quarry focus, rural operations
  
- **Victoria** (`victoria.html`)
  - Target keywords: Victoria loader scales, Melbourne region
  - Content: Agricultural, construction, quarry solutions

### 3. **Schema Markup** (Structured Data)
Added JSON-LD schema to help Google understand your content:

- **Organization Schema** (index.html): Identifies KMC as a business
- **LocalBusiness Schema** (each page): Shows service areas and locations
- Helps Google show rich snippets in search results

### 4. **Meta Descriptions**
Each page has unique, keyword-rich meta descriptions (155-160 chars) designed to:
- Include location + industry keywords
- Match search intent
- Encourage clicks from search results

### 5. **Title Tags**
Optimized titles include:
- Primary keyword (loader scales)
- Industry or location
- Brand name (KMC)
- Example: "Mining & Quarry Loader Scales | KMC"

### 6. **Sitemap Updates** (`sitemap.xml`)
- Added all new pages (industry + location)
- Set proper priority levels
- Now 10 pages total (from 3)
- Helps Google crawl and index efficiently

### 7. **Internal Linking**
- Added navigation sections on index.html
- Each page links to related content
- Establishes information hierarchy
- Improves SEO juice distribution
